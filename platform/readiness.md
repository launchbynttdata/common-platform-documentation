# Readiness is the delivery path at the start of an engagement

## Responsibility

At the start of an engagement, before substantial business functionality is in place, the platform team builds the path the application team will use to deliver a change. Readiness is that path. The practice page lists what must be true before the application team relies on the platform to deliver its work.

## Boundary

Readiness is a practice. There is no separate product for it, and there is no public repository for it. The practice page holds the list of what must be true. For a particular engagement, access, pipelines, and environments are recorded in that engagement's repositories.

## Where the behavior lives

The practice is [Establish a minimum platform path at the start](../practices/sprint-zero-platform-readiness.md).
