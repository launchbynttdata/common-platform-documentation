# Archive

**This is not current guidance.**

Material under `archive/` is historical context. It is not a principle, a practice, a standard, or a platform map entry. Do not copy commands, configuration, or screenshots from the archive into a delivery path. Do not treat an archived page as a team default.

The knowledge base links here so the archive can be found. Active topic navigation does not list archived documents.

## What belongs here

Procedures, diagrams, and notes that are no longer how the team works, when they still explain an older decision. Archived text may keep older product names. It must not keep credentials, account or tenant identifiers, personal filesystem paths, or private contact details.

## What does not belong here

- Current team defaults. Those stay in the active sections.
- Empty directories that only reserved a title. Those are removed, not archived.
- Private sources and machine-local paths.

## Editorial status

Archived pages are not active guidance. They are not held to the four questions used for principles, practices, standards, and the platform map (the problem, the position, the expectation, and where implementation details live).

## Disposition

[Content disposition](content-disposition.md) defines the four outcomes for legacy material: rewritten, retained, archived, and removed. It records which path families were moved into this archive and which were deleted.
